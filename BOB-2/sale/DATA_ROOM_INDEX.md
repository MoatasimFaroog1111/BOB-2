# Buyer Data Room Index

Never place live passwords, tokens, private keys, unredacted customer data, or production database exports in the data room. Grant access in stages and log every recipient.

## 01 — Executive summary

- Product overview and one-page investment memo.
- Founder background and reason for sale.
- Transaction structure and assets included/excluded.
- Asking price and valuation rationale.
- Buyer transition and support proposal.

## 02 — Product

- Current feature matrix and product boundaries.
- Screenshots, demo video, and sanitized demo credentials.
- Architecture and data-flow diagrams.
- Product roadmap and known limitations.
- Supported Odoo versions and tested workflows.

## 03 — Technology and source code

- Repository access and release commit/tag.
- Build, test, deployment, rollback, and recovery instructions.
- Backend/frontend test reports and CI history.
- Infrastructure inventory and monthly cost schedule.
- Dependency locks, SBOM, vulnerability reports, and third-party notices.
- Open issues, technical debt, and incident history.

## 04 — Security and privacy

- Security architecture and threat model.
- Tenant-isolation, RBAC, MFA, secret-management, audit-chain, and backup evidence.
- Penetration-test or vulnerability-assessment reports.
- Subprocessor and data-location inventory.
- Retention, deletion, incident-response, and breach-notification procedures.
- Credential and ownership transfer/rotation plan without secret values.

## 05 — Legal and intellectual property

- Incorporation, commercial registration, VAT information, and authorized signatory.
- Copyright/IP assignments and contributor agreements.
- Trademark/domain ownership.
- Proprietary license and third-party license review.
- Privacy notice, DPA, SaaS terms, support/security schedule, and counsel approvals.
- Customer/vendor agreements and transfer/consent requirements.

## 06 — Finance

- Monthly P&L, balance sheet, and cash flow.
- Revenue by customer and plan.
- MRR/ARR bridge, churn, refunds, bad debt, and deferred revenue.
- Hosting, software, payroll/contractor, sales, and support expenses.
- Bank/payment-processor evidence with sensitive details redacted.
- Tax filings and liabilities.

## 07 — Customers and growth

- Customer list with anonymized summary for early-stage access.
- Cohort retention, activation, usage, and churn metrics.
- Traffic and acquisition-channel analytics.
- Sales pipeline and partnerships.
- Support volume, SLAs, and customer concentration.
- References or testimonials with permission.

## 08 — Operations and transition

- Team/contractor roster and transferable agreements.
- Owner operating procedures and weekly time allocation.
- Monitoring, incident, backup, and disaster-recovery runbooks.
- Railway, GitHub, domains, email, analytics, and vendor transfer checklist.
- 30/60/90-day buyer handover plan.

## Access stages

1. Public listing: non-confidential product and high-level metrics.
2. NDA access: detailed financial/customer/technical summaries.
3. LOI access: contracts, security evidence, infrastructure costs, and deeper metrics.
4. Confirmatory diligence: source, production evidence, legal originals, and transfer plan.
5. Closing: credentials are rotated and transferred through approved secure channels only.
